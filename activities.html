Online Activity

Verb Detective: Find the Verb!

Read short sentences and identify the verbs. Use your phone, tablet, or computer. No materials needed.

How to Play

Welcome to Verb Detective! Read each sentence on the screen, find the word that shows an action or state of being, and select the correct answer. Click "Check Answer" to see if you are right. Continue until you finish all five questions.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify verbs in simple sentences.
  • Choose the correct verb based on the meaning of a sentence.
  • Use verbs correctly in your own sentences.
