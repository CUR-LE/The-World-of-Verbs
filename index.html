Why Are Verbs Important?

They help us express actions and activities.

They make sentences complete and meaningful.

They help us communicate our thoughts and ideas.

They tell us what people, animals, and things do.

They make our stories and conversations more interesting!

Remember:

"Verbs are action words that make our sentences meaningful!"
